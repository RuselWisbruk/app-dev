### Island

> Island is my favorite K-drama because of its unique combination of dark fantasy, supernatural action, and Korean mythology
> I really enjoy the mysterious characters, especially Van, because of his powerful abilities, tragic past, and calm personality.
> What makes it special to me is how it combines intense demon-hunting battles with an interesting storyline that keeps me hooked from beginning to end.
> Island is a fantasy-action series about Jeju Island, where evil supernatural creatures threaten humanity.
